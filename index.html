Questions, answered.

Who can join Freelance PH?

Any Filipino freelancer, virtual assistant, creative, specialist, consultant, or remote worker. Beginners are just as welcome as veterans.

Is it really free?

Yes. Creating your profile and getting listed is free. Founding Members keep their badge for good.

I'm new to freelancing. Can I join?

Absolutely. You don't need years of experience or a huge portfolio. Add the links you have and build from there.

Do I need a website?

No. A website helps, but you can use any professional link you have: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Behance, GitHub, or a Google Drive folder of samples.

What happens after I submit?

We review your profile and email you once it's live, usually within 48 hours. If something needs fixing, we'll tell you what and why.

Who can see my contact details?

Only what you choose to show. You decide which contact details and links are public, and you can change them anytime.

Does Freelance PH guarantee clients or income?

No. We make you easier to find. Whether a conversation turns into work is up to you and the client.

Is this like Upwork or OnlineJobs.ph?

No. Freelance PH is a directory, not a marketplace. There's no bidding, no proposals, no ratings, and no fees. People find you and contact you directly.

I live outside the Philippines. Can I join?

If you're a Filipino freelancer, you're welcome. You can list your current country or city on your profile.
